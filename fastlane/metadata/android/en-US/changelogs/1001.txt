Gridlink Mail 1.0.0. The interface is finished; this is the release it was heading for.

- Reading: choose whether opening a message marks it read, or leave that to the swipe and the button.
- Filters can now ask about more than one thing at a time, and act on more than one.
- Mail tags are read from the server itself, so tags made in another client show up here.
- Contacts read as facts, not as a form nobody can type in, and can be deleted from the edit screen.
- Calendar: tapping a day takes the reading pane back, and scrolling the agenda moves the month grid with it.
- Off switches are drawn in ink, so an off setting stops reading as an unavailable one.
- Attachments open and save against the message's own account, and photos in the formats phones actually shoot.
- Forms keep their pane when the keyboard is up, instead of being squeezed into the last inch of the screen.
- Around 700 new tests covering every screen.
