Sterna now handles shared mailboxes. If someone has given your account access to theirs, or you run several accounts on the same server, each one appears in the drawer with its own folders, its own notifications and its own Sent copies, and a shared mailbox says so plainly under its name. Replying from a shared mailbox sends as that mailbox, not as your login. Replies got smarter about which of your addresses they come from: a message addressed to one of your aliases is answered from that alias, with its signature, and the participants panel now names the address the message came in on. Nothing is guessed, so a mailing list or a blind copy still uses your default identity. Opening a message from a notification now takes you to it properly: the app switches to the message's account and its folder, so going back leaves you in a list that actually contains what you just read. A snoozed message coming due no longer ignores your settings, so it stays as private on the lock screen as you asked and stays quiet during your quiet hours, while still arriving even if new mail notifications are off for that account, because you asked for that reminder yourself. Deleting a queued message now asks first, since it has not been sent and nothing else holds a copy. Elsewhere: the encryption warning is no longer hidden behind the keyboard; the "-- " line above your signature can be turned off, and it is shown while you type the signature so you do not add a second one; conversations no longer flicker as they open; deleting the folder you are standing in returns you to the inbox; Back closes the search instead of minimising the app; swiping right can open the drawer when you have no action assigned to it; and the overflow menu is narrower and lines up the same way in every folder.
