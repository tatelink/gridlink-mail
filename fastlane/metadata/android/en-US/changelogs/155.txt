Emoji now keep their real colours in dark mode, in the subject list and the message. Reopening a draft or starting a reply places the cursor in the message body, ready to type. A new grouped setting marks messages as read when you delete, archive, or move them out of the inbox, whichever you pick. The inbox refreshes on its own the moment your connection comes back, even behind a VPN. Rotating the screen no longer reopens a message you had tapped from a notification. Adding a filter rule and backing out no longer leaves an empty rule behind, and the destructive Empty trash now sits at the bottom of its menu.
