Drafts can now be opened and edited: tap a draft to reopen it in the composer, attachments included, and sending replaces the saved copy. Messages deleted from another client no longer linger in the app, and archiving an unread message no longer notifies you about your own move. The reader gains an Archive button and keeps its toolbars still while you swipe between messages. The add-account screen holds the whole sign-in block above the keyboard and always offers a way forward after restoring a backup. OpenPGP switches can be turned off even when OpenKeychain is gone, and the mailbox menu puts the frequent actions first.
