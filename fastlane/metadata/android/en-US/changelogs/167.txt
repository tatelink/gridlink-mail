Selecting mail

- During a search, Select all now takes the results on screen instead of the whole folder. Filtering, selecting all and deleting no longer empties the folder.
- With the unread filter on, Select all takes the unread messages, not the read ones behind them.
- Snoozed messages are no longer selected: they are not on screen.

Reading

- A message you send to yourself now reads by its sender in the Inbox, in the list, inside an unfolded conversation, and in the reader. Sent, Drafts and Trash are unchanged.
- A new setting removes the Reply and Forward buttons at the bottom of a message. Reply stays in the toolbar at the top, Forward in its menu.

Writing

- The prompt you get when leaving the composer now offers Cancel, so you can go back to what you were writing.
- It no longer says it will discard the message when a saved draft stays on the server: it says it drops your changes.
- Save draft is greyed out when the composer is empty, because saving nothing would delete the draft.
- A draft can be deleted from the composer itself. It asks first, then the draft goes to the Trash and can be brought back.

Sync

- A folder larger than one server page now syncs. The "Everything" setting no longer leaves a large folder unsynced behind a permanent "You're offline".
- A conversation with more members than the server serves at once now opens instead of showing nothing.
- Local search indexing sizes itself on what the server accepts.
- A refresh that fails for a reason other than the network no longer says you are offline: it says the sync failed, and shows why.
