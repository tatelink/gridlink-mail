Gridlink Mail 2.1.0.

- Filter rules now work on IMAP accounts, on servers with ManageSieve.
- Fixes a battery drain when an IMAP server dropped the connection.
- Switch accounts by tapping the address at the top of the menu.
- New colour presets in the menu: Graphite, Deep Blue, Ember, Forest and Sand.
- Set how see-through the home-screen widgets are.
- Import contacts from a .vcf file.
- Works with PGPony and other OpenPGP apps as well as OpenKeychain.
