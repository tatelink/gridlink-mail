Makes the app stop claiming things it has not done. A message deleted from another device could stay in your list until you cleared the cache; it may still show up briefly, but the app now re-checks by itself and the row goes. Emptying the Trash keeps a message you pull back out during the undo window, no longer cancels itself when you tap it twice, and on IMAP it clears the whole folder on the server rather than only the part you had scrolled through. Folders and attachments named in Cyrillic (or any non-Latin script) show their real name and still open. If your VPN drops and comes back, the app notices and syncs on its own. Notifications set to show neither sender nor subject no longer offer buttons for a message you cannot identify, and the outbox dot stays quiet for a send that is going out normally. In Settings, tapping a row quickly no longer opens the same page twice, the page changes are shorter and stop moving altogether if you have turned animations off in Android, and picking a provider in Quick setup can now be undone, with Yandex and Mail.ru added. Leaving a message you reopened from the outbox now says what actually happens to it. Scrolling a message no longer flips to the next one when your finger drifts sideways. Contact badges follow your system palette.
